# Noor Parfums - English Edition

1. All website text, buttons, modals, and checkout are now completely in English.
2. Direct Gallery Upload:
   - Click 'Add Perfume (Admin)'
   - Click 'Choose from Gallery' to upload perfume photos directly from your phone.
3. Edit / Delete:
   - Click pencil (Edit) or trash (Delete) icons on any perfume card.
4. WhatsApp Orders:
   - Change '923001234567' in index.html to your real WhatsApp number to receive customer orders directly.
5. How to Publish for Free:
   - Visit https://tiiny.host or https://app.netlify.com/drop
   - Upload index.html and publish your live link!
